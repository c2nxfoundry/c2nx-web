Accessibility

Accessibility statement

We design to WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

C2NX wants everyone to be able to use this site. Our standard is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA.

What we do

  • Semantic page structure, a skip link and full keyboard navigation with visible focus.
  • Text and interface colors chosen for contrast on dark backgrounds.
  • Animation respects your system's "reduce motion" setting.
  • No content depends on color alone.

Feedback

If something on this site is hard to use, email contact@c2nx.com and we will respond within two business days.
