Most operational failures don't come from a lack of effort. They come from information that arrives late, in pieces, to the wrong person.

I've spent my career in security and operations, where that gap decides outcomes. C2NX exists to close it, one problem at a time. We pick a narrow, painful bottleneck, prove that people will pay to fix it, then build the fix as a durable company with its own name and its own customers. If a venture can't earn its place, we stop it early and say so.
